Activity: design your first workflow

SAMPLE COURSE MATERIAL

Your task

Write a five-line workflow for responding to a learner who asks when their class begins.

  1. Identify the request.
  2. Find the relevant course calendar entry.
  3. Draft a concise answer containing the date and time zone.
  4. Compare the draft with the source.
  5. Record whether the answer is ready to send.

Success criteria

Your workflow includes a source of truth, a human review and a way to handle missing information.
