TERTIARY LEARNING

Privacy policy

Tertiary Learning is operated by Tertiary Infotech Academy Pte Ltd for registered learners and trainers. Last updated: 7 September 2026.

Information used

The app uses your registered email, name, account identifier, assigned classes and course-material links to provide access to the learning portal. The portal stores enrolment and teaching records. When you enable reminders, your Apple push device token is linked to your portal account.

Security and sharing

Verification codes are single-use and expire after 10 minutes. Session tokens are stored in the iOS Keychain. Apple processes push delivery; the notification text does not include your course title or personal details. Your configured email provider delivers OTP messages. Course materials may open on Google Drive or other provider-hosted services subject to their own policies.

The app does not use advertising, cross-app tracking or third-party analytics. Feedback opens a WhatsApp draft; nothing is sent until you send it. Adding a class to Apple Calendar is optional and uses Apple’s calendar editor.

Control and deletion

Disable notifications in iOS Settings. Sign out to revoke your session and unregister this device. Account → Delete account permanently removes your shared portal login credentials and profile identity, revokes sessions and stops reminders. Your provider retains training, attendance, assessment and financial records required for its training administration and statutory obligations; these are not erased by deleting your portal login. Contact the provider regarding retention or access requests.
