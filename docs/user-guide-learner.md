# Learner User Guide

Complete guide for learners using AI-LMS-TMS.

[Back to Home](./)

---

## Overview

As a **Learner**, you can enroll in courses, track your learning progress, submit assessments, and earn certificates. The platform provides an intuitive interface with AI-powered assistance to support your learning journey.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In

1. Navigate to the AI-LMS-TMS platform
2. Enter your registered email address
3. Click **"Send OTP"** to receive a verification code
4. Enter the 6-digit OTP sent to your email
5. Click **"Verify & Login"**

### First-Time Setup

After your first login, complete your learner profile:

1. Click on your profile icon in the top-right corner
2. Select **"Profile"**
3. Fill in your personal details:
   - Full Name
   - NRIC/FIN (for Singapore residents)
   - Contact Number
   - Date of Birth
   - Nationality
   - Employment Status
   - Company (if employed)
4. Click **"Save"** to update your profile

## Courses

### Viewing Your Courses

1. Click **"Courses"** in the navigation menu
2. Browse your enrolled courses in grid view
3. Use filters to narrow down courses:
   - **Course Type**: WSQ, IBF, or General
   - **Mode of Learning**: Classroom, Online, or Blended
4. Use the search bar to find specific courses

### Accessing Course Content

1. Click on a course card to open it
2. View course details:
   - Course description
   - Learning outcomes
   - Duration and schedule
   - Trainer information
3. Access course materials:
   - **Learner Guide**: Course handbook
   - **Slides**: Presentation materials
   - **Resources**: Additional learning materials

### Learning Units & Subtopics

Courses are organized into **Learning Units** containing **Subtopics**:

1. Click on a Learning Unit to expand it
2. View the list of subtopics
3. Click on a subtopic to access its content
4. Mark subtopics as complete when finished

### Bookmarking Content

Save important content for later:

1. Click the **bookmark icon** on any subtopic
2. Access bookmarked content from your course view
3. Click again to remove the bookmark

### Tracking Progress

Your progress is automatically tracked:

- **Progress Bar**: Shows completion percentage
- **Completed Units**: Green checkmarks indicate completed items
- **Remaining Content**: Track what's left to complete

## Assessments

### Viewing Assessments

1. Navigate to your course
2. Click on the **"Assessments"** tab
3. View all assessments for the course:
   - Assessment name and description
   - Category (Written, Oral, Practical)
   - Deadline
   - Status (Not Started, In Progress, Submitted, Graded)

### Assessment Categories

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Written** | Essays, reports, case studies |
| **Oral** | Presentations, verbal examinations |
| **Practical** | Hands-on demonstrations, projects |

### Submitting Assessments

1. Click on the assessment you want to submit
2. Read the instructions carefully
3. Prepare your submission:
   - Write your response (if text-based)
   - Upload files (if file submission required)
4. Click **"Submit"**
5. Confirm your submission

> **Note**: Some assessments may only allow one submission. Check the requirements before submitting.

### Viewing Submission History

1. Go to the assessment
2. Click **"View Submission"** or **"Submission History"**
3. See your submitted work and feedback
4. View grades when available

## Certificates

### Earning Certificates

Certificates are awarded when you:

1. Complete all learning units
2. Submit all required assessments
3. Achieve passing grades (if applicable)

### Downloading Certificates

1. Go to your completed course
2. Click **"Download Certificate"**
3. Save the PDF certificate to your device

### Certificate Information

Your certificate includes:
- Your full name
- Course title
- Completion date
- Training provider details
- Certificate number (for verification)

2. Contact **Help & Support** for technical issues
3. Reach out to your **trainer** for course-related questions

## Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

**Can't log in?**
- Check your email address is correct
- Ensure you're using the latest OTP (they expire after 30 minutes)
- Contact support if issues persist

**Can't see my courses?**
- Verify you're enrolled in the course
- Check with your administrator if enrollment is pending
- Refresh the page

**Assessment won't submit?**
- Check file size limits (usually 5MB)
- Ensure you've completed all required fields
- Try a different browser if issues persist

**Progress not updating?**
- Refresh the page
- Clear browser cache
- Log out and log back in
